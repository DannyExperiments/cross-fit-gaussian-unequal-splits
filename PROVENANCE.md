# Provenance

The original conjecture appears in §3.1 of Dunn, Ramdas, Balakrishnan and Wasserman and in Dunn’s dissertation. This manuscript revises the [recovered proof](proof/recovered-construction.md) and includes the [unequal-split expansion](proof/unequal-split-expansion.md). The release adds exact finite checks and an illustrated example.

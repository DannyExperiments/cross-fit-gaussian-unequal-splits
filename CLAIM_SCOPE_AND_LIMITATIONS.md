# Claim scope

The sample is IID Gaussian with arbitrary mean and known identity covariance. The two nonempty folds are fixed, or selected independently of the data conditional on their sizes. The cross-fit set thresholds the equally weighted arithmetic mean of the two opposite-fold likelihood ratios. The objective is expected squared Euclidean diameter.

The strict finite counterexample uses four observations, dimension one and nominal noncoverage level exp(−36). The exact balanced formula uses dimension at least two. The limiting slope and exponentially accurate unequal expansion fix the fold sizes and dimension while α tends to zero. The optimal split at ordinary nominal levels, expected volume, testing power, many-fold aggregation and data-dependent splitting are outside the claim.

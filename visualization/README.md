# Bounding the cross-fit diameter

Extract the ZIP and open index.html in a browser. The explorer is self-contained. Rotate the inner and outer balls, choose the split shown, and adjust the distance between its means. Change the log threshold or dimension to compare the sampling expectations for the two splits.

At log threshold 36 in dimension one, the expected squared diameter is at least 120 for a 2–2 split and below 104 for a 1–3 split. The geometry uses a fixed distance between means as an illustration. The expectations average over all distances.

Why the region lies between these balls

The confidence set accepts a candidate mean when the average of the two ratios is below eL. Both ratios below eL guarantee inclusion. Either ratio reaching 2eL guarantees exclusion. The two facts give an inner ball centered at the midpoint and an outer ball centered at the mean of the larger testing fold. With equal folds, either fold gives the outer ball.

The 1–3 expectation bound uses log 2 < 1. The 2–2 entry is a lower bound in dimension one and an exact formula in dimensions two and three. Lower bounds shown as decimals round down.
